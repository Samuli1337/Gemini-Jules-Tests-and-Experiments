## 📝 Proposal Structure Overview

An ERC Starting Grant application consists of two main parts: **Administrative Forms (Part A)** and the **Scientific Proposal (Part B1 & B2)**, plus mandatory annexes.

### 1. Part A – Administrative Forms (Online / Submission Portal)
Filled out directly in the online submission system:
- **Section 1: General Information** (Acronym, Title, Abstract, ERC Review Panel, Keywords)
- **Section 2: Participants & Host Institution** (PIC number, contact persons)
- **Section 3: Budget Breakdown** (Direct personnel costs, travel, equipment, indirect costs 25%)
- **Section 4: Ethics & Security** (Ethics self-assessment table)
- **Section 5: Call-specific Questions** (Eligibility, PhD date, extension requests)

---

### 2. Part B1 – Evaluated in Step 1 & Step 2 (PDF Upload, Max 5 pages + 2 pages CV/Track-record)

**Target Audience:** Evaluators from your primary ERC panel (broad domain experts).

* **Section a. Extended Synopsis** *(Max 5 pages)*
  - Concise description of the groundbreaking nature, ambition, and scientific approach of the project.
  - Feasibility, key objectives, and potential impact.
* **Section b. Curriculum Vitae & Track-Record of the Principal Investigator (PI)** *(Max 2 pages)*
  - Standardized CV covering education, career breaks, key research outputs, publications, awards, grants, and leadership achievements.

---

### 3. Part B2 – Evaluated in Step 2 Only (PDF Upload, Max 14 pages)

**Target Audience:** Expert peer reviewers and panel members during full proposal evaluation & interview.

* **Section a. State of the art and objectives**
  - High-level research vision, addressing current limitations in the field.
* **Section b. Methodology**
  - Detailed scientific methodology, experimental design, risk management, and work plan.
* **Section c. Resources (including project budget breakdown & justification)**
  - Justification of personnel, equipment, consumables, travel, and direct/indirect costs.

---

### 4. Mandatory Annexes

* **Host Institution Letter of Commitment**
  - Signed by the legal representative of the Host Institution confirming binding support for the PI and project.
* **PhD Certificate & Eligibility Extension Proof** *(if applicable)*
  - Proof of PhD defense date (2–7 years prior to reference date; extended for maternity/paternity, illness, national service, etc.).

---

## 💡 How to Start Working on Your Application

1. **Review Guidelines:** Read `information_for_applicants_stg_cog.pdf` and `erc_work_programme_2026.pdf` to confirm eligibility (PhD date window: 2 to 7 years post-PhD).
2. **Draft Part B1 First:** Focus on the Extended Synopsis (5 pages) and CV/Track-Record (2 pages) as these determine passing Step 1.
3. **Draft Part B2:** Detail the methodology and budget breakdown (14 pages).
4. **Coordinate with Host Institution:** Secure the signed Host Institution Letter of Commitment early.
